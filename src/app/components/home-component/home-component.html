Angular Navigation

Angular uses a powerful Router module to navigate between views. You can define routes, use routerLink, and manage navigation seamlessly in single-page applications.

Template Driven Forms

Template Driven Forms rely on directives like ngModel and ngForm. They are simple to use and ideal for small-scale forms.

Reactive Forms

Reactive Forms provide a model-driven approach using FormGroup and FormControl. They are scalable, testable, and best for complex applications.
